How it works

A small helper runs on your Mac. It keeps Instagram’s Messages page open in a hidden browser tab, signed in to a spare account that only you message. There is no server and no Instagram developer app.

Share a reel with that account and the helper downloads it, reads the caption, a few frames and the speech, and asks Claude Code on your Mac what the reel points at. It finds the real source, checks the files and messages you back. Nothing is installed until you reply yes.

About a minute from your DM to “done”

4 secondsFirst it notices your DM. The Messages page stays open on your Mac.
10 secondsThen it watches the reel: the caption, a few frames and the speech.

2 secondsLast, on your yes, it copies the files in and says done.

Questions before you install

Does Instagram allow this?

No. reelstoclaude drives a normal account in a browser, which is against Instagram’s terms, and that account can get locked. Use a spare one. If Instagram asks to confirm you are human, reelstoclaude stops and shows a Mac notification. It never tries to get past the check.

Does it run code from a reel?

No. A skill is copied in as files and a repo is downloaded; nothing from either is run. Before you say yes, the reply tells you how many scripts a skill contains.

Who can send it reels?

Only you. It reads one chat, the one with your own account, and ignores message requests, follow requests and every other chat.

What leaves my Mac?

Speech is turned into text on your Mac. The caption, the transcript and a few frames go to Claude through your own Claude Code sign-in. There is no reelstoclaude server.

What does it cost?

Early access is free. Each reel uses a little of your Claude plan: about two cents of API-equivalent usage on Haiku in our tests, less for a funny reel. Waiting for messages uses none. A paid plan may come later; no price is set.

How heavy is it?

It keeps one hidden browser tab open on the Messages page so it can answer within seconds. On our Mac that is about 1 GB of memory.

Windows or Linux?

Not yet. It is built for macOS: Homebrew for the tools and a login item for the background helper.
